TCM Formula Groups & Molecular Mechanisms

Source: §4 of the paper

Representative formulas are categorized into four mechanistic groups (emphasizing each formula's predominant mechanism while acknowledging their multi-target network nature):

  1. Mitochondrial homeostasis & autophagy modulators
  2. Cellular senescence & inflammatory-microenvironment modulators
  3. Stem-cell maintenance & epigenetic reprogramming modulators
  4. Gut microecology & systemic-metabolism modulators

Group 1 — Mitochondrial Homeostasis & Autophagy

  • Zuogui Pill (ZGP) / Yougui Pill (YGP): classic kidney-tonifying prescriptions (nourish kidney yin / warm kidney yang). In C. elegans, both extend healthspan via PINK1/Parkin-mediated mitophagy. Organ-specific: in AD mice ZGP inhibits Drp1-mediated mitochondrial fission, activates AMPK/PGC-1α biogenesis; in ovary/testis reduces oxidative damage, restores mitochondrial membrane potential; in joints (YGP) promotes chondrocyte autophagic clearance. ZGP also maintains ovarian/BMSC stemness via Notch1/Nrf2 and SIRT1, reducing SASP and DNA damage.
  • Kaixin San (KXS): targets neuronal mitochondrial homeostasis + gut–brain axis. In brain: PINK1/Parkin autophagy in AD mice; in polyQ C. elegans: heat-shock response + autophagy clears polyQ aggregates; ginsenoside components support folate metabolism via DHFR → SIRT3 upregulation; attenuates neuroinflammation and inhibits ferroptosis (NLRP3/caspase-1/GSDMD, SIRT1/FSP1). In AD patients, restores gut-microbiota composition → cognitive improvement.
  • Guiqi Shen (GQS): modern oral liquid. The paper traces it to the Ming-dynasty formula Guilu Erxian Jiao, later refined with Astragalus and Salvia; qi-blood replenishing, yin-yang harmonizing. Guilu Erxian Jiao itself is the separate formula GEG, below. In chronic fatigue syndrome: markedly effective rate 55.56%; regulates intestinal oxidative stress → intestinal stem-cell homeostasis. Key constituents: ginseng acidic polysaccharide WGPA-A (reverses mitochondrial structural degeneration in striated muscle; ↓MDA/LDH, ↑SOD/GPX); ginsenoside Rg1 (microglial mitophagy via PINK1/Parkin, inhibits NLRP3); astragaloside IV (AMPK activation, mitochondrial function).

Group 2 — Cellular Senescence & Inflammatory Microenvironment

  • Buyang Huanwu Decoction (BYHWD; cerebrovascular/cardiovascular use): targets the SASP–inflammation axis — SIRT1 activation suppresses SASP factors (IL-1β, TNF-α, IL-6), downregulates p16/p21; modulates TLR4/NF-κB/NLRP3, CaM/CaMKII/MAPK, p53/cGAS/STING cascades. Uniquely: exosomal miR-590-5p → SLC8A3 reprograms macrophages M1→M2. Also: AMPK/Drp1 mitochondrial dynamics; SIRT1/AMPK cerebral glucose metabolism; MMP-9/TIMP-1 ECM normalization.
  • Liuwei Dihuang Wan (LWDHW, classic Kidney-Yin-nourishing formula): multi-organ anti-aging network centered on senescence + chronic inflammation: ovary (SIRT1/PI3K/AKT), testis (AMPK/SIRT1/NF-κB), bone (YAP-autophagy axis in BMSCs, osteogenic differentiation), brain (microglial inflammation, cAMP/PKA/CREB), vasculature (DNMT1-mediated ERα methylation inhibition → endothelial protection), autophagy (ATG5/Beclin1/LC3B via PI3K/Akt/FoxO3a), gut (restores Lactobacillus, ↓Proteobacteria).

Group 3 — Stem Cell Maintenance & Epigenetic Reprogramming

  • Bazi Bushen Capsules (BZBC): randomized trial in 530 healthy adults: telomerase activity +76.7% ≈ 5-year physiological age reversal; in aged mice reversed DNA-methylation age, upregulated skin stemness markers (OCT4, SOX2, NANOG, COL17A1); SIRT3-dependent DHRS2 deacetylation links epigenetics to mitochondria; suppresses NLRP3 + p16/p21; gut-microbiota–spermidine axis (enriches Akkermansia muciniphila); inhibits microglial inflammatory polarization.
  • Guilu Erxian Jiao / GEG (replenish essence, benefit marrow): directly targets haematopoietic stem cell (HSC) aging: inhibits p16 via histone methylation at the p16INK4a promoter → maintains HSC self-renewal; modulates SLAM–SAP pathway (T-cell IFN-γ control); restores chondrocyte autophagic flux + mitochondrial function via AMPK/mTOR/ULK1, PINK1/Parkin. Targets six hallmarks: stem-cell exhaustion, mitochondrial dysfunction, impaired autophagy, altered intercellular communication, inflammation, cellular senescence.

Group 4 — Gut Microecology & Systemic Metabolism

  • Sijunzi Decoction (SJZD, spleen-invigorating, qi-supplementing): restores gut homeostasis in aged rats (↑Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium + SCFAs) → alleviates systemic inflammation/oxidative stress; brain: upregulates Beclin-1/LC3-II hippocampal neuroprotection; skin: prevents UVB photoaging (↑collagen/elastic fibers, ↓MDA/ROS, ↑CAT/T-SOD); bone/hair: inhibits PI3K-AKT, p38 MAPK.
  • Yishen Huashi Granule (YSHSG, from Li Dongyuan's Shengyang Yiwei Tang; gut–kidney axis): in diabetic kidney disease: restores gut dysbiosis (↑Lactobacillus, ↓Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes), ↓intestinal permeability, ↓uremic toxins (indoxyl sulfate); ↑miR-339-5p inhibits TGF-β1/Smad → reduces renal fibrosis; improves mitophagy via mTOR/AMPK/PI3K/Akt.
