# Classical TCM Theories of Aging

*Source: [§3.1 of the paper](../sources/pmc-13620404-fulltext.md)*

TCM views aging as the gradual depletion of essential substances — **jing (essence), qi (vital energy), xue (blood), jin-ye (body fluids)** — plus the natural decline of zang-fu (organ) functions. The six core theories:

## Kidney Essence Depletion Theory (the most influential paradigm)

- The kidney stores **congenital essence**, the material basis of growth, development, and reproduction.
- Progressive kidney-essence decline → systemic deterioration: reduced fertility, bone fragility, cognitive impairment, loss of vitality.
- Molecular correlates (see [hallmarks mapping](hallmarks-mapping.md)): reduced **NAD+** pool (inhibits SIRT1-mediated deacetylation repair and PARP1-dependent DNA damage response → genomic instability, telomere attrition), and downregulated **Klotho** (disrupts Wnt/β-catenin in bone-marrow mesenchymal stem cells → stem cell exhaustion).

## Kidney Deficiency and Blood Stasis Theory

- Integrates **deficiency** and **stagnation** into a bidirectional vicious cycle: deficiency promotes stasis; stasis exacerbates deficiency.
- Kidney deficiency reduces NO bioavailability, raises ET-1 → microvascular spasm, reduced erythrocyte deformability → chronic hypoxia + extracellular-matrix sclerosis.
- Hypoxia + damage-associated molecular patterns activate the **NLRP3 inflammasome** (IL-1β/IL-18 release → low-grade inflammation).
- Hypoxia + inflammatory factors upregulate **TGF-β1/Smad**, driving fibroblast→myofibroblast transition and breaking the MMPs/TIMPs balance → collagen cross-linking and matrix sclerosis.
- **HIF-1α** (hypoxia regulator) inhibits mitochondrial respiratory chain complex I/IV (↓ATP), increases lysosomal membrane permeability, impairs autophagosome–lysosome fusion.

## Qi-Blood Theory

- Qi and blood form an **energy-material couple**: qi = bioenergetic flux/signalling; blood = oxygen/nutrient delivery.
- Aging shifts them from prosperity to decline:
  - Qi deficiency raises the **AMP/ATP ratio**, reducing insulin/IGF-1 sensitivity → disturbs the **AMPK–mTORC1 nutrient-sensing axis** (deregulated nutrient sensing).
  - Blood deficiency → insufficient perfusion of haematopoietic and mesenchymal stem cells (stem cell exhaustion).
  - Qi stagnation → poor blood flow hinders neurotransmitter/cytokine transmission between neurons and glia (altered intercellular communication).
  - Long-term qi-blood deficiency activates the **Rb/p16INK4a** pathway → irreversible G1 cell-cycle arrest (cellular senescence), reduced metabolic flexibility and social-function withdrawal (psychosocial isolation).

## Spleen-Stomach Theory

- Spleen-stomach = the "**postnatal foundation**" for nutrient transformation and distribution.
- Age-related decline (small-intestinal villous atrophy, gut dysbiosis) → impairs qi/blood generation → accelerates multi-organ senescence.
- Mechanisms: destroyed intestinal tight junctions → LPS translocation → **TLR4/MyD88** activation in liver → metabolic endotoxemia; compensatory AMPK activation; insufficient **leucine and short-chain fatty acids** → impaired 4E-BP1/S6K1 phosphorylation (loss of proteostasis); mitochondrial β-oxidation/TCA flux limits → mtDNA mutations (genomic instability).

## Heart-Kidney Interaction Theory & Liver-Governing-Dispersion Theory

- **Heart-kidney interaction:** dynamic balance between the two organs sustains youthful function.
- **Liver-governing-dispersion:** emotional regulation and unobstructed qi movement delay aging.
