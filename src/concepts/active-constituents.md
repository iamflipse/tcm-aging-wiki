# Bioactive Constituents & Multi-Compound Synergy

*Source: [§5 of the paper](../sources/pmc-13620404-fulltext.md)*

The anti-aging effect of TCM formulas is mediated by bioactive ingredients across several chemical categories.

## Major compound classes

- **Saponins** — amphiphilic (hydrophobic aglycone + hydrophilic sugar chains); embed into cell membranes, bind membrane cholesterol, regulate membrane proteins. The most extensively studied anti-aging class.
- **Flavonoids & glycosides** — C6-C3-C6 skeleton; polyphenolic electron-donating + metal-chelating → free-radical scavenging, redox signalling.
- **Polysaccharides** — high-MW polymers; fermented by gut microbiota into short-chain fatty acids; systemic (not single-cell) anti-aging effects via microbiota, immune aging, stem-cell exhaustion.
- **Organic acids & small molecules** — carboxyl/phenolic groups, high polarity; penetrate membranes and join core metabolism (TCA cycle, fatty-acid oxidation).

## Representative compounds (by class)

### Saponins
- **Ginsenosides Rg1 / Rb1** (KXS, SJZD): activate **AMPK/mTOR** and **AMPK/SIRT1** → mitophagy + mitochondrial function; systemic anti-aging across brain, heart, kidneys, testes, muscle.
- **Astragaloside IV** (BYHWD, GQS): activates **Parkin-mediated autophagy** (mitochondrial quality control); improves HSC mitochondrial function via **PGC-1α**; targets ketohexokinase → intestinal stem-cell proliferation.
- **Notoginsenoside R1**: inhibits **NLRP3 inflammasome**, lowers SASP factors.

### Flavonoids
- **Kaempferol** (ZGP/YGP): activates PINK1/Parkin mitophagy in *C. elegans* (healthspan extension); balances BMSC osteogenic vs adipogenic differentiation (postmenopausal osteoporosis).
- **Quercetin** (BYHWD): suppresses SASP, ↓p16/p21, activates SIRT1 → vascular/cardiac aging.
- **Icariin** (BZBC): prenylated flavonoid; skin photoaging via **KEAP1-NRF2**; vascular endothelium via **PI3K/Akt/eNOS**.

### Polysaccharides
- **Astragalus polysaccharide** (BYHWD): enhances umbilical-cord MSC proliferation, modulates immune network (in vitro + in vivo).
- **Angelica sinensis polysaccharide** (Si Wu Tang): protects neural stem cells, preserves HSC/progenitor function in aged mice.
- **Panax ginseng polysaccharide** (SJZD): remodels gut microbiota, enriches beneficial fatty-acid metabolism, activates AMPK/SIRT1 → suppresses low-grade inflammation.
- **Rehmannia glutinosa polysaccharide RGP70-1** (LWDHW): promotes **DAF-16** nuclear translocation → antioxidant/anti-inflammatory defence.

### Organic acids & small molecules
- **Ferulic acid**: activates Nrf2, suppresses NF-κB (cognitive decline); PINK1/Parkin mitophagy + SIRT1 → cardiac aging.
- **Chlorogenic acid** (Yinqiao San): inhibits NLRP3 → gut-dysbiosis-induced inflammaging.
- **Tanshinone IIA**: neuroprotection in aged POCD rats via **Nrf2/SLC7A11/GPX4** axis; ↓hippocampal inflammatory factors; inhibits ferroptosis.

## Multi-compound synergy (three modes)

1. **Pharmacokinetic synergy** — polysaccharide particle aggregates (e.g., *Coptis* decoction) enhance intestinal absorption/bioavailability of poorly soluble co-administered compounds (berberine); concentration-ratio-dependent synergy (Jinshui Chenfei: isoliquiritin + glycyrrhizic acid + gallic acid suppressing macrophage TNF-α/IL-1β/IL-6/CD206/Arg-1).
2. **Pharmacodynamic synergy** — BYHWD exemplar: astragaloside IV (SIRT1-mediated autophagy/mitochondria) + flavonoids (SASP suppression, ↓p16/p21) + exosomal miR-590-5p→SLC8A3 (M1→M2 macrophage reprogramming) — three mechanisms covering autophagy, senescence, and immune microenvironment simultaneously.
3. **Toxicity-modulating synergy** — SJZD's *Atractylodes macrocephala* (↑Occludin/ZO-1 tight-junction proteins, ↑Lactobacillus) + *Poria cocos* (mucosal repair, ↓permeability, ↑SOD/CAT, insulin/IGF-1 pathway (daf-2, age-1, daf-16)) — alleviates GI irritation from licorice-type tonic herbs; unifies toxicity reduction with efficacy enhancement for safe long-term use.
