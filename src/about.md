## What this wiki covers

This wiki distills the paper into six concept pages (theories, hallmark mapping, formula groups, constituents/synergy, methods, challenges/roadmap) and keeps the **full text as a source of truth** under [sources/](sources/pmc-13620404-fulltext.md).

## Scope note

The paper is a 2026 systematic review bridging classical TCM gerontology with the 14 modern hallmarks of aging; it categorizes representative formulas into four mechanistic groups, and proposes AI/multi-omics methods and a translational roadmap. The wiki's concept pages track the paper's section numbers (e.g. §3.1, §4.2.1) so every distilled note can be traced back to the source.
